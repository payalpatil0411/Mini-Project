About E-Tech

E-Tech is an E-Waste Management System that promotes proper handling and disposal of electronic waste.

It helps users understand e-waste and encourages them to recycle, reuse and dispose of electronic devices responsibly.

What is E-Waste?

E-waste means electronic products that are old, damaged or no longer being used.

Examples of E-Waste

  • Mobile phones
  • Laptops and computers
  • Televisions
  • Printers
  • Keyboards and mice
  • Batteries

Our Aim

The main aim of E-Tech is to create awareness about e-waste and encourage people to manage electronic waste in a safe and responsible way.
